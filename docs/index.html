Example historical episode

Jan 30, 2025 — Flash Flood / Flood / High Wind

In the research notebook, this episode showed roughly 27% cancellations and 42% severe disruption, with an observed operational recovery proxy of about 39 hours. It is a useful example of why extreme events need a confidence/severity layer in addition to nearest-neighbor matching.
